# Legal And Safety Notes

## Allowed By Default

- Read public YouTube channel RSS feeds.
- Download publicly available captions/transcripts.
- Save metadata, URL, summaries, and short citations with links to original videos.

## Avoid

- Downloading full video by default.
- Downloading audio without a clear need and permission.
- Bypassing login, subscriptions, paywalls, members-only restrictions, DRM, private videos, or unavailable content.
- Reproducing long transcript passages verbatim.

## Local Transcription Fallback

Use local transcription only when:

- the user owns the content or has permission to process it;
- the content was supplied by the user;
- there is a clear lawful basis for processing it.

Always mark local transcription as potentially error-prone.
